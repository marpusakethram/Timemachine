# Brag Plan: Time Machine

## What is this app?
A decision lab for founders. You drag a slider and see what a raise, a hire or
bootstrapping does to your runway and your ownership, all in your browser.

## The angle
Founders talk about runway. They rarely look at their own stake while they do it.
The video opens on the number nobody wants to watch shrink, founder ownership,
then shows the app making that trade-off visible in one drag.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A giant "100%" with "You own the company." Then: "What does a raise really cost?"

## Key moments
- The live model panel: dragging Capital raised from $200k to $400k while founder
  ownership counts down from 83.3% to 71.4% and the cash line climbs.
- Three path cards land one by one: Bootstrap 19 months, Hire 6 months,
  Raise "Beyond 24 mo" at 83.3% ownership.

## Outro / punchline
TM logo lock-up. "Free, open source, runs in your browser." plus the GitHub address.

## User flow worth showing
Open the demo, pick Raise, drag the capital slider, compare the three paths.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: calm B2B finance product film, ledger look
- Interpretation: few scenes, long readable holds, one simulated drag as the
  centerpiece, restrained sound.

## Format: landscape, 1920x1080
## Duration: 21s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f3f4f1 (canvas), panels #fbfbf9
- Accent: #2f4bd8 (cobalt)
- Text: #15191f, muted #47505c
- Display font: Schibsted Grotesk
- Body font: IBM Plex Sans
- Strongest visual element: the home page live model with the big ownership number

## Share copy (draft)
Before you raise, drag the slider. Time Machine shows what a round does to your
runway and your stake, right in the browser.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10 (110 BPM), low under the visuals, fade out at the end
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Strong cues 15.82, 18.01, 18.55, 20.19. Beat grid
  for the cards: 14.20, 15.28, 16.38 (every other beat so each card can be read).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass makes the hero accent glow breathe slightly
- SFX posture: sparse; soft impact on the reveal, light clicks on the drag,
  one card slide per path card, a bell on the logo
- Restraint rule: no SFX on every beat, nothing over the hook text

## Storyboard

### Scene 1: Hook, 3.55s
Giant "100%" with "You own the company." At ~1.9s: "What does a raise really cost?"
Sequential/interaction: two lines, each held well past reading time.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2: Reveal, 3.8s
TM logo square + "Time Machine", then "Don't guess. Move the assumptions."
Audio: soft impact as the logo lands.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3: The drag, 6.3s
Recreated live model panel (Fictional SaaS, Bootstrap / Hire / Raise tabs, ownership,
runway, cash at month 24, cash chart, Capital raised slider). A cursor grabs the
slider and drags $200,000 → $400,000. Ownership counts 83.3% → 71.4%, cash at
month 24 climbs, the chart line rises. Caption: "Drag the capital. Watch your stake move."
Sequential/interaction: simulated drag with light clicks.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4: Compare, 4.4s
"Three paths. Side by side." Cards land one by one on the beat grid:
Bootstrap conservatively, 19 months, 100.0%; Hire two engineers, 6 months, 100.0%;
Raise $200k, Beyond 24 mo, 83.3%.
Sequential/interaction: three cards, card slide on each.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5: Outro, 3s
TM logo locks at 18.55 (strong cue). "Free, open source, runs in your browser."
github.com/marpusakethram/Timemachine. Music fades under the end.

**Music mood:** upbeat but restrained
**Audio summary:** a light bed that swells into the card reveals and lands on a bell with the logo.
